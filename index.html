考研秘書 Kaoyan Secretary

考研秘書是一個只給作者本人使用的 Mac 小工具，在本機執行，用來整理作者自己的 Gmail：

  • 把新信分類、貼標籤，把電子報與通知移出收件匣
  • 每天早上整理重要信件的摘要，傳到作者自己的 Discord 頻道
  • 作者在 Discord 說要寄什麼信，工具寫好草稿；作者確認並選好時間後才寄出

Kaoyan Secretary is a personal macOS tool used only by its author. It runs locally on the author's own Mac to organize the author's own Gmail inbox: it labels and archives incoming mail, sends a daily summary to the author's private Discord channel, and drafts emails that are sent only after the author approves them and picks a send time.

It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
